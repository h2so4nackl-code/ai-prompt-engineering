# Contributing

Suggestions, corrections, and practical improvements are welcome.

## Contribution guidelines

- Keep content model-agnostic unless a model-specific detail is necessary.
- Base technical guidance on a reproducible method or authoritative source.
- Label fictional or illustrative scenarios clearly.
- Do not include credentials, personal data, confidential information, or proprietary material.
- Do not claim benchmarks, results, or experience without verifiable evidence.
- Preserve the repository's practical, concise, and educational style.
- Check grammar, Markdown, and internal links before submitting a change.

## Suggested process

1. Open an issue describing the proposed correction or improvement.
2. Keep each pull request focused on one topic.
3. Explain what changed and why it improves the material.
4. Include any verification steps or sources needed to review the change.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository's MIT License.
