# Case Studies

Fictional and anonymized educational scenarios demonstrating structured analysis. They do not describe employers, customers, or confidential projects.

## Contents

- [AI Response Evaluation](case-study-ai-evaluation.md) — source-grounded assessment of a policy answer

The case studies emphasize method, evidence, uncertainty, and reusable lessons rather than fabricated results or performance claims.
